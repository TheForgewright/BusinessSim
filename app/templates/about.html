About Business Simulation Game

A comprehensive web-based business simulation and economic game designed for classroom use.

Game Overview

Students manage companies, invest in stocks, and compete for the highest final score over a predetermined number of turns. The game features complex economic mechanics including facility management, capital generation, stock markets, and debt management.

Core Mechanics

  • Capital System: Manage 4 types of capital (Goods, IP, Influence, Labor) plus Cash
  • Facilities: Build physical and personnel facilities to generate capital
  • Weekly Turns: 29-phase turn processing with depreciation, maintenance, and generation
  • Stock Market: Dynamic pricing based on company equity and performance
  • Debt Management: Take loans with interest accrual, compounding, and auto-pay
  • Product Tagging: Create products with market positioning tags
  • Market Competition: Compete based on product overlap and saturation

Winning

Final Score = Company Equity + Personal Cash

The student with the highest final score at game end wins!
